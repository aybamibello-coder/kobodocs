Frequently asked questions

What is a WHT credit note and why does it matter?

When a client deducts withholding tax from your invoice before paying you, they're required to issue a WHT credit note as proof of the deduction. You need these to offset the deducted amount against your own tax liability at filing time — losing track of them means losing that credit.

How is the 21-day remittance deadline calculated?

The remittance due date is 21 days from the date the deduction was made, and any record within 5 days of that deadline is flagged on your dashboard so you can follow up on the credit note before it's overdue.

Does KoboDocs submit anything to FIRS or the NRS on my behalf?

No. WHT Tracker is a record-keeping tool only — it logs deductions and generates a certificate PDF for your own files. It does not file or submit anything to FIRS/NRS; actual remittance and filing still happen through your normal tax process.

Can I link a WHT record to an invoice I created on KoboDocs?

Yes — if the payment came from a KoboDocs invoice, you can link the WHT record to it so your gross amount matches automatically. You can also log deductions from payments invoiced outside KoboDocs.
