Is this actually legal in Nigeria?

Yes. Nigeria's Evidence Act (section 93) and the Companies and Allied Matters Act both recognise electronic signatures as legally binding, provided they can be attributed to the signer. Every signature on KoboDocs is timestamped and IP-logged as part of an audit trail attached to the document.

Frequently asked questions

Is an e-signature legally valid in Nigeria?

Yes. Nigeria's Evidence Act (section 93) and CAMA both recognise electronic signatures, and courts have upheld them as binding when properly attributed to the signer.

Does the person I send a document to need a KoboDocs account?

No. Each signer gets a unique link by email and signs directly in their browser — no signup or login required on their end.

What documents can I send for signature?

Any PDF — contracts, invoices, tenancy agreements, quotations, or anything else you generate on KoboDocs or upload yourself.
