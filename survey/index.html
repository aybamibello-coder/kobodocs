Frequently asked questions

Is KoboDocs Survey free to use?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 3 surveys and 100 responses a month, with up to 10 questions per survey — no card required.

How is this different from SurveyMonkey?

The biggest difference is pricing: SurveyMonkey's plans and per-response caps are billed in USD and get expensive fast once you cross a small response threshold. KoboDocs Survey is priced in Naira, has no per-response overage fees, and tells you when you're approaching your plan's limit instead of surprise-billing you or cutting off responses.

Can I run a Net Promoter Score (NPS) survey?

Yes — NPS (0–10 scale) is a built-in question type, alongside Likert agree/disagree scales, star ratings, and ranking questions, so you don't need a separate tool for CSAT vs. NPS vs. general research.

Can I measure employee satisfaction anonymously?

Yes — share the survey link without collecting names or emails, and responses come back without identifying who submitted them, which is standard practice for honest internal feedback.

What happens if I go over my monthly response limit?

You're notified as you approach your plan's limit so you can decide whether to upgrade — you won't be charged a per-response overage fee or have your survey suddenly stop collecting responses.

Can I export survey responses to Excel?

Yes — every response can be downloaded as a CSV file and opened directly in Excel or Google Sheets for your own analysis.
