Frequently asked questions

What changed in the 2026 tax reform?
Nigeria's Tax Reform Acts, effective 1 January 2026, raised the small-company exemption threshold from ₦25 million to ₦100 million annual turnover (with total fixed assets of ₦250 million or less). Qualifying businesses are exempt from Company Income Tax, Capital Gains Tax, and the 4% Development Levy.

Does this exemption cover VAT and PAYE too?
No — the exemption is specifically for Company Income Tax, Capital Gains Tax, and the Development Levy. VAT and PAYE obligations still apply regardless of turnover; track those on our free Compliance Calendar.

Is this free?
Yes — checking your exemption status is free, with no login required.
