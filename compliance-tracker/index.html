Why Nigerian businesses miss compliance deadlines

Every registered business has recurring CAC, tax, and pension obligations — most SMEs currently track them with spreadsheets, WhatsApp reminders to themselves, or an accountant's memory.

Missed deadlines mean penalties, not just paperwork. A late CAC annual return or a lapsed license can cost far more to fix than the compliance itself — and it's usually invisible until a bank, investor, or regulator asks for proof you're in good standing.

Frequently asked questions

What is business compliance tracking software?

Business compliance tracking software keeps a calendar of a company's recurring legal and regulatory obligations — CAC annual returns, tax filings, pension remittances, license renewals — and reminds the business owner before each deadline, instead of relying on memory, spreadsheets, or an accountant's calendar.

Does Compliance Tracker file my CAC annual returns or taxes for me?

No. Compliance Tracker tracks deadlines, stores your documents, and reminds you before each one is due — it doesn't submit filings to CAC, FIRS, or any other agency on your behalf. You still file directly or through your accountant; Compliance Tracker makes sure nothing is forgotten.

How much does Compliance Tracker cost?

Compliance Tracker is included in KoboDocs Business Suite, starting at ₦15,000/month on the Starter plan. There's a 21-day free trial and no card required to start.

Can my accountant or lawyer use Compliance Tracker too?

Yes. Business owners can invite their accountant, lawyer, HR lead, or finance team as team members with their own role, and an accountant managing several client businesses can see all of them from one place.

Is the AI compliance assistant a substitute for a lawyer or accountant?

No. The AI assistant answers plain-English questions grounded in your own business's tracked obligations and documents, but it gives general guidance, not legal or tax advice — always confirm specifics with a licensed professional.
