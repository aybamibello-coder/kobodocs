Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Every free account gets 10 minutes of transcription per month, no card required. Create a free account to start — upgrade only if you need a larger monthly allowance or saved history.

What file types can I upload?

Common audio and video formats are supported, including MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4, and MOV.

Can I transcribe from a URL or link instead of uploading a file?

Yes — paste a direct link to an audio or video file, such as a podcast episode URL, and it transcribes without any upload. Support for pasting YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram links directly is coming soon; for now, download the audio from those platforms first and upload it.

Does it identify different speakers?

Yes. Speaker identification is included, labeling each speaker in the transcript so you can see who said what.

What languages can I translate a transcript into?

Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Nigerian Pidgin, Fulfulde and Kanuri, plus French, German, Portuguese, Spanish, Arabic and Swahili. Pro and Business plans get unlimited translations; free accounts get one full translation per month.

Can I get subtitles for a video?

Yes. Subtitle generation in SRT and VTT format is available on the Pro and Business plans.

Is there a pay-as-you-go option, or do I have to subscribe monthly?

Both. Prepaid minute packs starting at ₦1,500 for 60 minutes never expire and don't require a subscription. Monthly plans are available if you transcribe regularly.

How is usage measured?

By actual audio/video duration processed. A failed transcription doesn't consume any of your minutes.
