Frequently asked questions

Do my customers need an account to book?

No. They open your link, pick a service and time, and enter their name and phone number. No password, no download.

Can I take payments through the booking page?

Not through KoboDocs directly — price is shown as information only. This keeps the free and lower-cost plans genuinely free of payment-processing risk. If you need to collect a deposit, share your own payment link separately.

Does it work if I have more than one staff member?

Yes, from the Growth plan up — each staff member gets their own working hours and calendar, and you assign services to whichever staff member handles them.

What happens if two people try to book the same slot?

Only one succeeds — the moment a booking is confirmed, that time slot is removed for everyone else, so double-booking isn't possible.
