Frequently asked questions

How is PAYE calculated on KoboDocs Payroll?

Using the 2026 Nigeria Tax Act PAYE bands (7% to 24%), with the Consolidated Relief Allowance applied and pension/NHF deducted before arriving at taxable income — the same method used for statutory compliance. See the worked example in our PAYE guide.

What happens if I don't remit PAYE correctly?

Under the 2026 Nigeria Tax Act, under-deducted PAYE carries a penalty of up to 40%, and late pension remittance carries a 10% surcharge. Automating the calculation reduces manual errors that trigger these penalties.

Can I try Payroll before subscribing?

Yes — try the free single-payslip generator first, no login required. The paid plans above add employee management, monthly bulk runs, and stored payslip history for a whole team.
