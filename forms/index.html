Frequently asked questions

Is KoboDocs Forms free to use?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 3 forms and 100 submissions a month with no card required — enough for a school admission drive, a single event, or testing the builder before you commit to a paid plan.

How is this different from Google Forms?

KoboDocs Forms is built specifically for Nigerian use — pricing in Naira instead of a USD-billed Workspace plan, ready-made templates for school admissions, NYSC documentation, and church/mosque registration, and a WhatsApp share link alongside the usual web link and QR code. Paid plans also remove KoboDocs branding and add conditional logic and an AI form generator.

Can I collect file uploads, like ID documents or photos?

Yes — file upload is one of the 11 built-in field types. Uploaded documents are stored securely and visible only to you, which makes it suitable for vendor onboarding, ID verification, or admission documents.

Can I build an event registration or church registration form?

Yes. Event RSVP and church/mosque registration are both ready-made templates, and you can share the finished form as a link, WhatsApp message, or a printed QR code for in-person sign-ups.

What happens if I go over my plan's submission limit?

You won't be cut off mid-campaign. KoboDocs tells you when you're approaching your plan's monthly submission limit so you can upgrade on your own terms, instead of charging a surprise per-response overage fee.

Can I charge respondents money through a KoboDocs form?

Not directly — KoboDocs never collects or holds money on your behalf. If you need to charge respondents (for example, an event ticket or vendor deposit), add your own payment link as a field on the form.
