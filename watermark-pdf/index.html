Common reasons people watermark PDFs

Draft documents

Mark a contract or report "DRAFT" before it's finalized, so it's never mistaken for the signed version.

Confidential business documents

Stamp "CONFIDENTIAL" across proposals, financials or internal reports before sharing.

Branding

Copy protection

Discourage unauthorized reuse of shared PDFs with a visible ownership mark.
