Frequently asked questions

Is this loan repayment schedule generator free?
Yes — generating a repayment schedule is free, with no login required.

What information do I need to generate a repayment schedule?
The loan amount, interest rate, repayment period, and repayment frequency — the tool calculates each installment and the full schedule from there.

Is this useful for informal loans between individuals?
Yes — a written repayment schedule is useful for both formal and informal lending, giving both parties a clear, agreed reference for what's owed and when.
