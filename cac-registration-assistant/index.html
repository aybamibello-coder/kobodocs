Have these ready before you start

Avoid restricted words (e.g. "Federal", "National", "Nigeria") unless you're authorized to use them, and avoid names that are too generic or too close to an existing registered name.

Be specific — "trading" or "business" alone is a common reason for a query/delay.

CAC requires a physical address — a P.O. Box won't be accepted.

Common reasons applications get queried or rejected

  • The name on the application doesn't exactly match the name on the ID provided.
  • Business description is too vague (e.g. just "business" or "trading").
  • A P.O. Box given instead of a physical address.
  • Expired ID, or a blurry/cropped scan.
  • Proprietor is under 18 with no adult co-proprietor.
Business Name Registration Brief

What this is — and isn't

This organizes what CAC's Business Name registration needs into one clean brief, and flags the mistakes that most often cause delays or queries — it doesn't file anything with CAC on your own behalf, and it doesn't check live name availability. Submit your application directly on the official CAC portal, using this as your reference while you fill it in.

Registering a Limited Company instead of a Business Name? CAC requires your Memorandum & Articles of Association to be prepared by a licensed legal practitioner — that's not something we (or any non-lawyer tool) can generate for you. Once you're incorporated, our Post-Incorporation Document Pack covers what comes right after — Board Resolution to open a bank account, Share Certificates, and statutory registers.

This is a planning aid, not legal advice. Confirm current requirements on CAC's official portal before submitting.

Frequently asked questions

Does this register my business with CAC directly?
No — it organizes and checks the details CAC requires (proposed names, owner details, business nature) to reduce the chance of rejection, then you submit through CAC's own portal.

Is this for a Business Name or a Limited Company?
This assistant is built for Business Name registration. For a Limited Company, the Memorandum and Articles of Association must be prepared by a licensed lawyer — see our Business Name vs Limited Company guide.

Is it free?
Yes — organizing your registration brief is free.
