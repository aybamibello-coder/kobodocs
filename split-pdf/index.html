How to split a PDF

  1. Select the PDF you want to split.
  2. Choose whether to extract a specific page range, or split every page into its own file.
  3. If extracting a range, type the pages you want — e.g. 1-3, 5.
  4. Tap Split PDF and download the result. Everything runs on your own device.

Common reasons people split PDFs

Pulling out one form

Extract just the page a bank or embassy asked for out of a longer scanned document.

Sharing a chapter or section

Send only the relevant pages of a report instead of the whole file.

Separating a scanned bundle

Split a multi-document scan back into individual files, one per page.

Shrinking an upload

Remove pages you don't need before uploading to a portal with a size limit.

Frequently asked questions

Is splitting a PDF on KoboDocs free?

Yes. Splitting and extracting pages from a PDF is completely free, with no account required.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Splitting happens entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to KoboDocs servers for this operation.

Can I choose which pages to extract?

Yes. Enter a page range like 1-3 or a list like 1,3,5 to pull out exactly the pages you need as one new PDF.

Can I split every page into its own file?

Yes. Choose "Split into individual pages" and you'll get a ZIP file containing one PDF per page.
