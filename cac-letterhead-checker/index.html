What the law actually requires

Under CAMA 2020 sections 304(1), 304(2), and 729(1)(c), every company registered in Nigeria must display its registered name, RC number, and director details on business letters — and CAC has confirmed this extends to invoices and quotations too. The requirement isn't new; CAC simply began actively enforcing it from 1 August 2026, warning that non-compliant companies will face sanctions.
