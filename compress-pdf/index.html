Frequently asked questions

Is compressing a PDF on KoboDocs free?

Yes. Basic compression is completely free, with no account required.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens entirely in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to KoboDocs servers for this operation.

How much smaller will my PDF get?

It depends on the file. Text-based PDFs (documents, forms, exported files) usually shrink a noticeable amount. PDFs that are mostly scanned photos may only shrink a little with this free, browser-based tool, since deep image recompression needs heavier processing.

Why didn't my file get much smaller?

If a PDF is already well-optimized, or is made up mostly of high-resolution scanned images, browser-based compression has limited room to work with. This is an honest limitation of client-side processing, not a bug.
