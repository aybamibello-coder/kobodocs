Before you sign

  • If this Power of Attorney will be exercised outside Nigeria, it generally needs to be witnessed or certified by a Notary Public.
  • An irrevocable Power of Attorney can't exceed 12 months under Nigerian law unless it's for valuable consideration or coupled with an interest.
  • Choose your attorney carefully — a general PoA gives very wide authority. A specific PoA limits exactly what they can do.
  • For high-value property, financial, or corporate matters, have a lawyer review this before you sign.
