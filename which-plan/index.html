Growth vs. Receivable Manager, in one line: Growth's collections tools are built into your Business Suite invoicing and quoting workflow — they're for businesses already running invoices and quotes through KoboDocs. Receivable Manager is standalone — it works whether you invoice through KoboDocs, a bank transfer, WhatsApp, or paper, and if you do use Business Suite to invoice, those invoices sync into Receivable Manager automatically. They're not mutually exclusive — some businesses use both.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between Business Suite Growth and Receivable Manager?

Growth's collections tools live inside your Business Suite invoicing and quoting workflow — aging and ledgers build up from the invoices and quotes you send through KoboDocs. Receivable Manager is a standalone tool: you log any balance a customer owes you, however you actually invoice them, and it still tracks aging, DSO and reminders. If you already invoice through Business Suite, those invoices sync into Receivable Manager automatically too.

Can I use Receivable Manager without Business Suite?

Yes. Receivable Manager works on its own — you don't need to invoice through KoboDocs at all. Just add outstanding balances manually as they come in.

Should I start with Starter or Growth?

Start with Starter if you mainly need invoicing, simple debt reminders, bookkeeping and inventory. Move to Growth once chasing debt becomes a real job on its own — full aging buckets, a customer ledger, promise-to-pay tracking and reusable proposal templates. You can upgrade from inside Business Suite at any time.

Do I need an account for the free tools?

No. Every free KoboDocs tool — invoices, receipts, quotations, and more — works without signing up. Nothing is saved unless you choose to.
