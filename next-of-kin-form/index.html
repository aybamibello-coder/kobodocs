What "next of kin" actually means

A next of kin declaration on a bank, employment, or school form is for contact and emergency purposes only — so the person named can be reached on your behalf. It does not by itself transfer any right of inheritance over your money or property; that's determined by a will, or by Nigerian inheritance law if there isn't one. Frequently asked questions

Does my next of kin automatically inherit my money or property?

No. A next of kin declaration on a bank, employment, or school form is for contact and emergency purposes only. It does not by itself transfer any right of inheritance — that is determined by a will, or by Nigerian inheritance law if there is no will.

Can I list more than one next of kin?

Yes — the Pro template supports a primary next of kin plus an alternate, in case your primary contact is unreachable.

What's the difference between a next of kin form and a guarantor's form?

A next of kin form names someone to be contacted on your behalf, usually a relative — it doesn't create any obligation for that person. A guarantor's form is different: the guarantor is personally agreeing to be held responsible if you default on rent, a loan, or another obligation.
