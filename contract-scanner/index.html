Why a second read matters

Most contracts that cause problems later look fine on a first read. A missing dispute-resolution clause, a one-sided termination clause, or a land sale missing Governor's consent language are the kind of gaps that are easy to miss and expensive to discover after you've signed.

What it checks

Nigerian-law patterns

Land Use Act consent requirements, Labour Act notice/termination rules, CAMA references where relevant.

One-sided clauses

Unfair indemnity, termination that only favours one party, penalty clauses that may not hold up.

Missing essentials

No dispute resolution or governing law clause, no IP ownership terms, unclear payment schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as having a lawyer review my contract?

No. Contract Scanner is an automated first read that flags common risk patterns in plain English — it does not replace a lawyer, and for anything high-value or high-risk you should still have one review the contract before you sign.

What kinds of contracts can I scan?

Any contract text you can paste in: freelance/consulting agreements, rent or tenancy agreements, land sale agreements, employment contracts, power of attorney documents, or general agreements. Select the closest type for a more targeted read.

What does it check for?

Nigerian-law-relevant patterns such as Land Use Act consent requirements for land transfers, Labour Act notice and termination rules, missing dispute resolution or governing law clauses, one-sided indemnity or termination terms, unclear payment terms, missing IP ownership clauses in freelance work, and automatic renewal without a clear opt-out.
