    How to merge PDF files

    1. Select two or more PDF files from your device, or drag them into the box above.
    2. Drag to reorder them on desktop, or use the up/down arrows on mobile — files merge in the order shown.
    3. Tap Merge PDFs. This runs on your own device, so it works even on a slow connection.
    4. Download the combined PDF, or share it straight from your phone.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is merging PDFs on KoboDocs free?

    Yes. Merging PDF files is completely free, with no account required and no limit on how many times you use it.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Merging happens entirely in your browser using your device's own processing power. Your PDF files are never uploaded to KoboDocs servers for this operation.

    Can I reorder pages before merging?

    Yes. Drag files to reorder them on desktop, or use the up/down buttons on mobile. The files will be combined in the order shown.

    Does this work on my phone?

    Yes. The tool works on Android and iPhone browsers. Use your phone's file picker to select PDFs from storage, downloads, or cloud apps.

    What if one of my PDFs is password protected?

    KoboDocs cannot merge a password-protected PDF while it's locked. Remove the password on your device first, then merge.
