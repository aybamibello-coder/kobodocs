    1. Select one or more JPG or PNG images from your device.
    2. Drag to reorder them on desktop, or use the up/down arrows on mobile — the PDF will follow this order, one image per page.
    3. Tap Convert to PDF. This runs on your own device.
    4. Download the PDF, or share it straight from your phone.
