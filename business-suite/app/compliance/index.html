Tax & Compliance Settings

Set this up once — it's added automatically to every invoice, quotation, and letter you generate.

Since 1 August 2026, CAC actively enforces CAMA 2020 s.304 & s.729(1)(c): every registered company's business letters, invoices, and quotations must display the registered name, RC number, and every director's current (and former, if any) name — plus nationality for non-Nigerian directors. Non-compliance carries sanctions.
About NRS e-invoicing: Nigeria is separately rolling out mandatory e-invoicing (medium businesses by July 2026, small businesses by July 2027) through the NRS Merchant Buyer Solution. Entering your TIN and VAT number below adds them to your invoices — it does not submit invoices to NRS. Real submission requires your business to individually register on the NRS e-invoicing platform (einvoice.nrs.gov.ng) for your own API credentials, which KoboDocs cannot obtain on your behalf.
