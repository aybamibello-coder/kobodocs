What this is — and isn't

This gives you a properly formatted starting point — an application letter, a sample acceptance letter format, a logbook sample you can extend, a weekly report, and a report outline. Your institution's ITF/SIWES coordinator or industry supervisor may have specific requirements — always confirm those and adjust accordingly.

This is a drafting aid, not an official ITF document. Signatures, stamps, and supervisor sign-off still need to happen through the proper channel.

Is this free?
A basic version is free; a paid pass covers the full report and logbook generation across the SIWES period.

Does this replace my institution's official logbook format?
Formats can vary by institution — confirm your specific institution's requirements before submitting a generated report.
