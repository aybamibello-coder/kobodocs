What this is — and isn't

This generates standard company secretarial documents from the details you provide — it isn't a substitute for a company secretary or lawyer for anything beyond routine paperwork, and it doesn't file anything with CAC. Statutory registers should be kept updated as your company's membership or directorship changes — treat this as a starting point, not a one-time formality.

This is a drafting aid, not legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

What's included in the Post-Incorporation Document Pack?
Board Resolution (commonly needed to open a corporate bank account), Share Certificates, and statutory registers, including a Register of Directors.

Does this file anything with CAC?
No — this generates the documents themselves. It doesn't submit or file anything with CAC on your behalf.

Do I need this if I already used the CAC registration assistant?
Yes — CAC registration and post-incorporation documents (like a Board Resolution for a bank account) are separate steps. Registration gets your company set up; these documents are typically needed right after, for banking and internal governance.
